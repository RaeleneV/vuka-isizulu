Ukulungele Ukuxhaswa Ngezimali?

"Ubucabanga ukuthi awukwazi ukufaka isicelo sokuxhaswa ngezimali ngoba awubhalisiwe ne-CIPC. Ingabe lokho kuyiqiniso ngempela?" Abahwebi abaningi abangakahleleki bacabanga ukuthi ukuxhaswa ngezimali akulona olwabo. Eqinisweni, abaxhasi abaningana baseNingizimu Afrika basekela ngqo amabhizinisi amancane angakahleleki nangakabhalisiwe, okuhlanganisa abahwebi basemgwaqweni nabathengisi.

Uxhaso Olungadingi Ukubhaliswa ne-CIPC

Ucabanga ukuthi awukwazi ukufaka isicelo soxhaso ngoba awubhalisile. Ingabe lokho kuyiqiniso ngempela?"

Uhlelo lwe-NYDA Grant Programme, oluqhutshwa yi-National Youth Development Agency, lusekela ngokukhethekile amabhizinisi asebenza ngokungakahleleki noma ngokusemthethweni—okuhlanganisa nabathengisi basemgwaqweni—inqobo nje uma ibhizinisi lihlose ukwenza inzuzo futhi linamandla okuba yimpumelelo kwezohwebo. Lutholakala kubanikazi bamabhizinisi abasha abaneminyaka ephakathi kuka-18 no-35, futhi ungafaka isicelo kunoma yiliphi igatsha le-NYDA, ku-inthanethi, noma ngokushayela ucingo ku-0800 58 58 58. Uhlelo lwe-SEDFA Township and Rural Entrepreneurship Programme—olwakhiwe ngokuhlanganiswa kwe-SEFA, i-SEDA, kanye ne-CBDA—lugxile kakhulu emabhizinisini asem townships nasemakhaya, futhi lusekela ukuguqulwa kwamabhizinisi angakahleleki abe ngawasemthethweni. Ama-stokvel namaqembu okonga akuwona "umxhasi osemthethweni," kodwa ayindlela ethembekile esekelwe emphakathini evumela abathengisi abaningi baseNingizimu Afrika ukuthi bathole izimali ezinkulu zokuthenga izimpahla noma imishini ngaphandle kwenzalo ephezulu.

Thola Umuntu Ongaxhumana Naye Mayelana Noxhaso

Thola indlela eyodwa yokuxhumana ne-NYDA noma i-SEDFA ongayisebenzisa ukubuza mayelana noxhaso lwebhizinisi elingakahleleki kahle (informal business). Qedela lokhu bese uthola: 💸 Ibheji elithi "Funding Aware" (Ulwazi ngoXhaso).

Imiyalelo Yomfundi

  1. Khetha inhlangano eyodwa:
    • NYDA, uma uneminyaka engu-18–35.
    • SEDFA, uma usebenza noma uhlela ukusebenza ibhizinisi elisezindaweni zasemakhaya noma zasemalokishini.
  2. Sebenzisa iwebhusayithi esemthethweni yenhlangano, inombolo yocingo, ulayini we-WhatsApp, noma i-imeyili ukuze uthole indlela efanele yokuxhumana nayo.
  3. Gcwalisa ifomu elifushane elingezansi.

Lungela Ukuthola Uxhaso Ngaphambi Kokuba Uludinge

"Ithuba lokuthola uxhaso livuleka kusasa futhi linomnqamulajuqu wamasonto amabili. Ungakwazi yini ukufaka isicelo ngesikhathi?"

Qala ukulungiselela manje, ngaphambi kokuba ufake isicelo. Ngokuvamile uzodinga umazisi wakho, ubufakazi bomlando wakho wokuhweba (yingakho incwajana yakho yokubhala kanye ne-akhawunti yasebhange kubalulekile; yibona ubufakazi ongabubonisa umxhasi), incazelo elula yokuthi uxhaso luzosetshenziselwani ("isiqandisi sesibili sokunciphisa ukonakala kokudla," noma "isaphulelo sokuthenga izimpahla eziningi ngesikhathi samaholide"), futhi, uma kuyimali ebolekiwe kunokuba kube wuxhaso olungabuyiselwa, ube nombono oyisisekelo wokuthi uzoyibuyisela kanjani.
