Mayelana ne-Vuka

Ukuvala igebe lolwazi losomabhizinisi bomkhakha wokudla.

Abanikazi bamabhizinisi amancane okudla abaningi bafuna ukwenza amabhizinisi abo abe semthethweni futhi bawakhulise, kodwa ulwazi abaludingayo luhlakazekile, kunzima ukuluthola, noma lubhalelwe uhlobo oluhlukile lwabantu. I-Vuka iletha lolu lwazi endaweni eyodwa ehleliwe futhi emahhala.

  • Kubhalwe ngokukhethekile kubathengisi bokudla abasebenzela emakhaya, kwiziqukathi, nakumadivayisi ahambayo.
  • Akukho ukungena ngemvume, akukho zindleko, kanye nenguqulo ye-PDF elandekayo ukuze ufinyelele ungaxhunyiwe ku-inthanethi.
  • I-Vuka iyinkundla esebenza ngaphandle kokudinga i-inthanethi. Nokho, ukuxhumana kwakho kokuqala nalesi sizindalwazi kufanele kwenzeke ngenkathi uxhumeke ku-inthanethi.
  • Uma usuqedile ukusebenzisa iwebhusayithi ngokugcwele, ungazinqamula ku-inthanethi kodwa uzoqhubeka nokukwazi ukufinyelela ngokugcwele kokuqukethwe kanye nemisebenzi yayo, okuhlanganisa nokufinyelela amafayela e-PDF.
  • Yakhiwe ngethuluzi lokubala inhloko-mali ukuze ukwazi ukwenza uhlelo lwezimali ngaphambi kokuba ufake imali.

Imibuzo Ebuzwa Njalo

Ingabe ngiyayidinga i-akhawunti ukuze ngisebenzise i-Vuka?

Cha. I-Vuka imahhala ngokuphelele ukuyisebenzisa futhi akudingi ukubhalise, ungene ngemvume, noma wenze i-akhawunti. Konke kuyatholakala khona manjalo.

Ingabe kudingeka ngikhokhele okuqukethwe?

Cha, zonke izinhlelo zohambo, izihloko, kanye nethuluzi lokubala inhloko-mali kumahhala ukusetshenziswa, futhi akukho zindleko ezifihliwe.

Ngingayithola kanjani lempahla?

Uhambo ngalunye lunenketho ethi "Download PDF" lapho ungayilanda khona imininingwane yalolo hambo, futhi ungaphinde ulande wonke amahambo ngesikhathi esisodwa ekhasini elithi "Download PDF Version" elitholakala ngaphansi kwekhasi. Lokhu kuwusizo uma unokufinyelela okulinganiselwe noma okubiza kakhulu ku-inthanethi.
