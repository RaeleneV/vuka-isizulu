Uhambo 10

Uyini Umnyakazo Wakho Olandelayo?

"Ukwenzile. Ubhalisiwe, uvunyelwe, futhi uhweba ngokusemthethweni. Ngakho kungani kungazelelwe kuzwakale sengathi kunesethi entsha yemithetho?" Ukuthola okusemthethweni kwakuyisinyathelo sokuqala. Ukuhlala ngokusemthethweni ngenkathi ukhula, ukuthatha abasebenzi, ukupakisha umkhiqizo wakho, ukuxosha abathengi abakhulu, ukuya ku-inthanethi, kuyisinyathelo sesibili, futhi kubamba ngisho nabanikazi bamabhizinisi abanolwazi bengalindele. Lolu hambo ludlula ezinguqukweni uma usuqala ukukhuphula, isisekelo sezomthetho abantu abaningi abasiphuthelwa, kanye nezindlela ezimbalwa zokudala zokukhula ezingahileli njalo ukuqasha isitolo sesibili.

Ingabe Kufanele Uqhubeke Chakuba Ngumnikazi Wedwa Webhizinisi (Sole Proprietor)?

"Usunesikhathi esingangonyaka usebenza ngegama lakho elibhaliswe kwa-CIPC. Ingabe lesi yisimo esifanele sokwenza izinto ezilandelayo?"

Abantu abaningi baqala njengabanikazi bebhizinisi bodwa (sole proprietor) noma njengenkampani ezimele elula (Pty Ltd), futhi lokho kulungile ekuqaleni. Kodwa njengoba ukhula, uqasha abasebenzi, ungena ezivumelwaneni ezinkulu, noma uletha uzakwethu ebhizinisini, indlela ibhizinisi lakho elihlelelwe ngayo iba yinto ebaluleke kakhulu. Inkampani ezimele (Pty Ltd) ihlukanisa impahla yakho siqu ezingozini zebhizinisi ngendlela ecacile kunokuba kwenza ukuba ngumnikazi webhizinisi wedwa, futhi ngokuvamile yiyo le ndlela amakhasimende amakhulu, amabhange, nabatshali-zimali abalindele ukuyibona ngaphambi kokuba bangene esivumelwaneni esibalulekile nawe.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Akudingeki ukuthi ukhansele ukubhaliswa kwebhizinisi bese uqala kabusha. Ungashintsha uhlobo lwebhizinisi noma uluhlele kabusha ngokusebenzisa i-CIPC njengoba ukhula; buza bona ngqo ukuthi ukushintsha uhlobo lwebhizinisi kuhilela ini.

Ukuqasha Ilungu Lokuqala Lethimba Lakho Ngokusemthethweni

"Usanda kuqasha umuntu wokuqala ozokusiza. Ngaphandle kokumkhokhela, yini umthetho okudingeka uyenze manje?"

The moment you pay someone to work for you, even part-time or family, you become an employer in the eyes of the law. You must register for UIF (Unemployment Insurance Fund) with the Department of Employment and Labour within a few days of hiring (Unemployment Insurance Act 63 of 2001; Unemployment Insurance Contributions Act 4 of 2002), and register for COIDA (Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases) too, which covers your employee if they're hurt on the job (COIDA, Act 130 of 1993). Both apply even if you only have one employee. The Basic Conditions of Employment Act (Act 75 of 1997) also sets minimum rules on things like working hours, leave, and pay slips.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Bhalisela i-UIF kanye ne-COIDA ndawonye uma uqasha umuntu wakho wokuqala, iningi labaqashi licabanga ngoyedwa kuphela bese libanjwa ngomunye.

Ukulungela Ukuthengisa: Lokho Abathengi Abakhulu Abakuhlolayo Ngempela

"Umthengi wezitolo uthi unesithakazelo, bese ecela isitifiketi sakho sokuphepha kokudla. Ingabe unayo into engaphandle kwe-CoA yakho?"

Isitifiketi sakho sokwamukelwa sikamasipala sikwenza uhwebe ngokusemthethweni, kodwa abathengisi abakhulu kanye nabasabalalisi abasemthethweni bavame ukufuna okwengeziwe ngaphambi kokuba bakufake ohlwini: ubufakazi bomkhuba wokuphepha kokudla oqhubekayo, ngezinye izikhathi uhlelo oluqashelwayo njenge-HACCP, kanye nokuvunyelwa kwe-NRCS uma umkhiqizo wakho usemathinini, ubhema, noma ugcwele i-vacuum. Abaningi baphinde basebenzise ukuhlolwa kwabo kwabahlinzeki. Akukho kwalokhu okwenzeka ngobusuku obubodwa, ngakho-ke kufanelekile ukuthola ukuthi yini umthengi othile ayidingayo ngaphambi kokuba usondele kuye, kunokuba kube ngemva.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Cela umthengi ongaba khona uhlu lwakhe lokuhlola lwabahlinzeki ngaphambi komhlangano wakho wokuqala. Lukutshela ngqo ukuthi yini okufanele uyilungise kuqala, esikhundleni sokuqagela.

Umthetho Wokupakisha Amabhizinisi Akhulayo Awunaka Kakhulu

"Usuqalile ukupakisha isosi yakho emabhodleleni anophawu lwebhizinisi lakho. Bewukwazi yini ukuthi lokho kwenza ube nesibopho ngokomthetho ngalokho okwenzeka kulelo bhodlela ngemuva kokuba lilahliwe?"

Uma usungumkhiqizi noma umnikazi womkhiqizo ofaka izinto ezipakishiwe emakethe ngobuningi obubonakalayo, imithetho yaseNingizimu Afrika ye-Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) ngaphansi koMthetho Wokuphathwa Kwemvelo Kazwelonke: uMthetho Wodoti (Waste Act) ingase ikuthinte (Waste Act 59 ka-2008; Imithetho ye-EPR, GN R.1184 ka-2020). Empeleni, lokhu kusho ukubhalisa neNhlangano Yomkhiqizi Enesibopho (PRO) evunyiwe nokufaka isandla ekuqoqweni nasekuvuseleleni kabusha izinto zakho zokupakisha. Amabhizinisi amancane kakhulu avame ukukhululwa kulo mthetho, kodwa imingcele incane kunalokho abantu abaningi abakucabangayo, ngakho-ke kufanelekile ukuhlola kunokucabanga ukuthi mncane kakhulu.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Sesha iNhlangano Yomkhiqizi Enesibopho (PRO) ehlobene nohlobo lwakho lokupakisha (ipulasitiki, iphepha, ingilazi, insimbi) bese ubuza bona ngqo ukuthi ingabe ubuningi bemikhiqizo yakho budinga ukuthi ubhalise yini.

Ukuvikela Igama Olakhile

"Umuntu othile kwelinye idolobha uqala ukusebenzisa igama elifana kakhulu nelakho. Ungakwazi yini ukumvimba?"

Ukubhalisa ibhizinisi lakho ne-CIPC kuvikela ubuwena obusemthethweni benkampani yakho (uMthetho Wezinkampani 71 ka-2008), kodwa akuvikeli ngokuzenzakalelayo igama lomkhiqizo wakho, ilogo, noma igama leresiphi ekukopishweni ngomunye umuntu. Ngenxa yalokho, udinga uphawu lokuhweba, olubhaliswe ngokwehlukana ngesigaba sezimpawu zokuhweba ze-CIPC (uMthetho Wezinkampani 71 ka-2008). Uma ibhizinisi lakho linegama, ukubukeka, noma umkhiqizo abantu abawaziyo kakade, uphawu lokuhweba yilokho okuvimba umncintiswano ekuhwebeni ngedumela lakho.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Ungasesha izimpawu zokuhweba ze-CIPC zibhalise ngaphambi kokufaka isicelo, ukuze uhlole ukuthi igama lakho alikathathwa.

Amathuba Avulwa yi-B-BBEE kanye neSitifiketi Sokukhokha Intela (Tax Clearance)

"Ikhasimende eliyinkampani enkulu lifuna ukufaka i-oda elikhulu, kodwa kuqala licela isitifiketi sakho se-B-BBEE kanye nobufakazi bokuthi uhambisana nemithetho yentela (tax clearance). Ingabe unayo noma yikuphi kukho?"

Izinkampani eziningi kanye nayo yonke iminyango kahulumeni kudingeka ukuthi ithenge kumabhizinisi anesimo esihle se-Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (B-BBEE) (uMthetho we-B-BBEE 53 ka-2003), nakubahlinzeki abahambisana nemithetho yentela. Uma imali engenayo yebhizinisi lakho ngonyaka ingaphansi komkhawulo wamabhizinisi amancane kakhulu akhululiwe (EME), kungenzeka ukuthi uyafaneleka ukuthola isitifiketi se-B-BBEE ngokuzenzakalelayo, noma kwesinye isikhathi kube yisifungo esibhaliwe (affidavit) nje, mahhala. Isimo Sokuhambisana Nemithetho Yentela se-SARS (okwakubizwa ngokuthi isitifiketi sokukhokha intela) sitholakala mahhala ku-inthanethi uma izindaba zakho zentela sezilungisiwe (SARS), futhi womabili la madokhumenti avame ukuba yinto yokuqala ecelwa ngumthengi omkhulu noma umuntu okhipha ithenda kahulumeni.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Lungisa Isimo Sokuhambisana Nemithetho Yentela se-SARS kanye nesifungo se-B-BBEE ngaphambi kokuthi udinge ukuba nakho, hhayi ngemuva kokuthi ithuba elikhulu selivele likhona.

Udayisela ku-inthanethi? Yazi izibopho zakho mayelana nemininingwane yamakhasimende

"Uqala ukwamukela ama-oda nezinkokhelo nge-WhatsApp kanye newebhusayithi elula. Bewazi yini ukuthi manje usunezibopho ezisemthethweni eziphathelene nemininingwane yomuntu siqu yamakhasimende akho?"

Uma nje uqoqa imininingwane yamakhasimende njengamagama, izinombolo zocingo, amakheli, noma ulwazi lokukhokha, ngisho nangokungahleliwe, uMthetho Wokuvikelwa Kolwazi Lomuntu Siqu (i-POPIA, uMthetho 4 ka-2013) usebenza kuwe. Awudingi izinhlelo eziyinkimbinkimbi, kodwa udinga ukugcina lolo lwazi luphephile, lusebenzisele kuphela lokho okutshele ikhasimende ukuthi uzolusebenzisela kona, futhi ukwazi ukulususa uma becela. UMthetho Wokuvikelwa Kwabathengi (uMthetho 68 ka-2008) uphinde unikeze amakhasimende aku-inthanethi amalungelo okubuyiselwa kwemali, ukulethwa, kanye namanani acacile okungadingeki ukuthi acabange ngawo endaweni yokugcina imali kuphela.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Bhala umugqa omfushane amakhasimende abonayo lapho e-oda, usho ukuthi uzoyisebenzisela ini imininingwane yawo. Lokho kukodwa kukubeka phambili kunamabhizinisi amaningi amancane.

Ukukhula Kushukumisa Iningi Lamabhizinisi Okudla Elingaqapheli

"Wonke umuntu ukhula ngokuvula isitolo sesibili. Kuthiwani uma lokho kungeyona inketho yakho engcono kakhulu?"

Ukuqasha indawo yesibili akuyona ukuphela kwendlela yokukhula, futhi kuvame ukubiza kakhulu. Ukupakisha ndawonye kuvumela esinye isikhungo esibhalisiwe, esivunyelwe yi-NRCS ukuthi sikhiqize umkhiqizo wakho ngezinga ngaphansi komkhiqizo wakho, ukuze ukwazi ukuhlinzeka abathengisi ngaphandle kokwakha ifektri yakho. Ikhishi eliyifu, eliphekela ukulethwa kuphela lisuka ekhishini lebhizinisi elihlanganyelwe, linciphisa ingozi yokuqasha kwakho kanye nabasebenzi uma kuqhathaniswa nesitolo sesibili. Amabhokisi okubhalisela kanye namamodeli oku-oda kusengaphambili akuvumela ukuthi ukhiqize ngokumelene nesidingo esiqinisekisiwe esikhundleni sokuqagela. Futhi selokhu kwethulwa i-African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), abanye abakhiqizi bokudla baseNingizimu Afrika bahlola ukuthumela kwamanye amazwe kwamanye amazwe ase-Afrika, yize lokhu kuletha izidingo zayo zemvume nezindinganiso ezifanele ukucwaningwa kahle ngaphambi kokuthi uzibophezele.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Ngaphambi kokusebenzisa imali endaweni yesibili ebonakalayo, khipha izindleko zokupakisha ndawonye noma ikhishi elihlanganyelwe lamafu. Ukuzibophezela kwezomthetho kanye nezezimali ngokuvamile kuncane kakhulu.
