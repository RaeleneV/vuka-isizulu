Vumela Ifoni Yakho Ikulethele Amakhasimende

"Ukudla kwakho kuyazidayisa uma abantu sebekunambithile. Kodwa bakuthola kanjani ngaphambi kwalokho?" Cishe wonke umuntu eNingizimu Afrika usevele uyayisebenzisa i-WhatsApp, futhi kumahhala ukuyisebenzisela ibhizinisi; kudingeka idatha kuphela, ebiza kancane kunemali yokushaya ucingo. Bambalwa kakhulu abathengisi bokudla abayisetha ngendlela efanele. Uma yenziwe ngendlela efanele, yenza ibhizinisi lakho libukeke likwizinga eliphezulu futhi ivumela amakhasimende ajwayelekile ukuthi a-ode kusenesikhathi noma abheke ukuthi yini ekumenyu yalolo suku.”

Setha i-WhatsApp Business

Kukhona ofuna uku-oda amapuleti angu-10 omhlangano we-stokvel. Bangakuthumelela umyalezo, noma kumele baqale ngokuzofika mathupha lapho uthengisela khona?"

Landa i-"WhatsApp Business" (hhayi i-WhatsApp ejwayelekile) ku-Play Store noma ku-App Store; kumahhala. Bhalisa usebenzisa inombolo yocingo—kungaba ikhadi le-SIM lesibili uma ufuna ukuhlukanisa izingxoxo zebhizinisi nezomuntu siqu, noma inombolo yakho esetshenzwayo uma ingaxhunyiwe ku-WhatsApp ejwayelekile. Qinisekisa inombolo usebenzisa ikhodi ethunyelwa nge-SMS, bese usetha i-Business Profile yakho: igama lebhizinisi, isigaba ("Food & Beverage"), incazelo emfushane ngalokho okuthengisayo, indawo osebenzela kuyo namahora okusebenza, kanye nesithombe esicacile sokudla kwakho, indawo odayisela kuyo, noma i-logo yakho.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Setha umyalezo wokubingelela (isibonelo: "Siyabonga ngokuthumela umyalezo ku-Thandi's Kota Corner! Sivuliwe ngoMsombuluko kuya kuLwesihlanu, ngo-6 ekuseni kuya ku-5:30 ntambama esiteshini samatekisi") ukuze amakhasimende athole impendulo ngokushesha, ngisho noma umatasa ukhonza amanye amakhasimende.

Yakha Ikhathalogi Amakhasimende Angayiphequlula

"Ikhasimende elivamile lifuna ukwazi ukuthi yini ekumenyu namuhla, ngaphandle kokukufonela. Libonani ngempela?"

Ngaphansi kwemenyu Yamathuluzi/Amathuluzi Ebhizinisi, khetha "Ikhathalogu" bese wengeza izinto zakho, isithombe, igama, kanye nentengo ngayinye. Yabelana ngenombolo yakho ye-WhatsApp noma isixhumanisi namakhasimende ngokubhala uphawu oluncane noma ebhodini lakho lemenyu endaweni yakho yokudayisa, ukuze abantu bakwazi ukuyigcina

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Gcina ikhathalogi yakho ibuyekeziwe lapho izinto zithengiswa noma amanani eshintsha. Ikhathalogi ephelelwe yisikhathi idala izingxoxo ezingezinhle, hhayi ukuthengisa.

Xhumanis i-Facebook ne-Instagram

"Umuntu uthola ukudla kwakho ku-Instagram. Ingabe angakuthumelela umyalezo ngqo kusuka lapho, noma kumele afune inombolo yakho ngokuhlukile?"

I-Facebook ne-Instagram zingakusiza ukuthola ikhasimende lakho elilandelayo, futhi ungazixhumanisa ngqo kuhlelo lokusebenza lwe-WhatsApp Business. Vula uhlelo lokusebenza bese uya ku-Tools, cindezela u-Facebook & Instagram, bese uqhubeka nokuxhumanisa inkundla oyisebenzisayo. Ngena ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Facebook noma ye-Instagram, khetha i-Page ofuna ukuyixhumanisa, bese ucindezela u-Connect WhatsApp.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHA: Awudingi ukusebenzisa zombili izinkundla ngesikhathi esisodwa. Qala ngaleyo esivele isetshenziswa kakhulu ngamakhasimende akho.

Thatha izithombe zokudla okwenza abantu bafune ukukudla

"Ingabe isithombe sokudla kwakho singenza umuntu ongamazi azizwe elambile, noma angavele asidlule ngokushesha?"

Isithombe sokudla kwakho sivame ukunquma ukuthi ikhasimende lifuna ukuzama isidlo sakho noma cha. Isithombe sibeka isimo sengqondo futhi senze abantu bazizwe belambile ngaphambi kokuba banambithe noma yini. Yingakho kubalulekile kumakhasimende ukubona izithombe zokudla kwakho, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi kumenyu yakho noma kukhathalogi yakho ye-WhatsApp. Izithombe zokudla okuhle zenza abantu bakhulume, babelane, baphawule, futhi babize abangani. Okuthunyelwe okunezithombe kuthola ukubukwa okuningi kakhulu kunokuthunyelwe okunombhalo kuphela (candyfoxstudio.com), ngakho isithombe esimnandi singadala intshisekelo enkulu kunanoma iyiphi incazelo.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Ukukhanya kwemvelo kwenza okungcono kakhulu esithombeni sokudla kunanoma yisiphi isihlungi (filter). Thatha isithombe eduze kwefasitela noma ngaphandle, hhayi ngaphansi kokukhanya okufiphele kwesitendi.

Ungalokothi uphelelwe yizinto ongazithumela

“Sekuphele amasonto amabili kusukela kokuthunyelwe kwakho kokugcina. Yini ekuvimbile ngempela?"

Ukuze ugcine izinkundla zakho zokuxhumana zisebenza ngaphandle kokuphelelwa yimibono, yabelana ngezikhathi zansuku zonke ezivela ebhizinisini lakho: ukulungiselela ukudla, ukuphendula imibuzo evamile yamakhasimende, ukubonisa izinto zakho zemenyu ezithandwa kakhulu, noma ukugubha izenzakalo ezibalulekile. Gcina "ibhange lemibono" efonini yakho, uhlu oluqhubekayo lwezitayela, amavidiyo ahlekisayo, nezithombe ezingasoze zalibaleka, ukuze uhlale unokuqukethwe okulungele ukukuthunyelwa.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Awudingi umkhiqizo omkhulu. Isithombe esisheshayo nesiqotho sesitolo sakho sangempela sivame ukudlula esicwebezelayo kodwa esijwayelekile.

Bona imibono ephakanyisiwe
  • Khombisa indlela olungisa ngayo ukudla kwakho okuthandwa kakhulu.
  • Faka isithombe semenyu yosuku noma sokudla okukhethekile okunikezwayo.
  • Phendula umbuzo ovame ukubuzwa, njengokuthi "Nivula ngesikhathi bani?"
  • Yabelana ngombono wekhasimende noma ubonge ikhasimende elijwayelekile.
  • Khombisa izithako ezintsha ezifika noma ezilungiswayo.
  • Gubha impumelelo ebalulekile, njengosuku olumatasa lokuhweba noma ukugcina unyaka owodwa webhizinisi.
  • Yabelana ngesithombe esikhombisa okwenzeka ngemuva kwezigcawu endaweni yakho yokudayisela noma ekhishini.
  • Faka isithombe sesikhathi esihlekisayo noma esijwayelekile esenzeke ngesikhathi sokusebenza.
