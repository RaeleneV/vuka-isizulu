Uhambo 07

Yenza Abantu Bakukhumbule

Kunezindawo ezimbili ezidayisa uhlobo olufanayo lwe-kota, ngentengo efanayo, emgwaqweni ofanayo. Kungani enye inemigqa yamakhasimende kanti enye ingenayo? Ukwakha isithunzi sebhizinisi (noma i-branding) yindlela abantu ababona futhi bakhumbule ngayo ibhizinisi lakho lokudla. Lokhu kwakhiwa yiyo yonke into oyidayisayo kanye nendlela osebenzisana ngayo namakhasimende, nsuku zonke.

Okushiwo ngempela ukwakha uhlobo lomkhiqizo (nokuthi kungani kubalulekile)

""Ake ngiyothenga amagwinya kulowo mama" noma "ake ngiyothenga amagwinya kwa-[igama lebhizinisi lakho]": yikuphi okwenza umuntu othenge kanye kuphela abe ikhasimende elihlala libuya?

Bangaphezu kuka-80% wabantu abathi ukwethenjwa yisizathu esikhulu esibenza bathenge, yingakho ukwakha isithunzi sebhizinisi kubalulekile kungakhathaliseki ukuthi ibhizinisi lakho lokudla likhulu noma lincane kangakanani (Cloudfusion, 2025). Ukuba nesithunzi esihle kuyasiza ekutheni abantu bakukhumbule, futhi abantu abalukhohlwa ibhizinisi elisebenzisa imibala efanayo kanye nokupakishwa okuqoqekile okufanayo ngaso sonke isikhathi (Dandu, 2015). Kuphinde kwakhe ukwethenjwa: uma amakhasimende ebona ukupakishwa okuhle negama elifanayo ngaso sonke isikhathi, azizwa ephephile ngokomzwelo ukuthenga kuwe; futhi njengoba kunabantu abaningi abathengisa ukudla, ukuhlala ukhumbuleka yikho okwenza abantu bakukhethe kunabanye. Ukwakha isithunzi sebhizinisi kubalulekile nasethimbeni lakho. Kwenza abasebenzi bazizwe beziqhenya ngokuba yingxenye yento yangempela ekhulayo, hhayi nje ukwenza umsebenzi omncane (Cloudfusion, 2025).

🎨 Brand Aware (ibheji)

Isihloko sesibili

Uma uhlobo lwakho (branding) luzama ukujabulisa wonke umuntu ngempela, ingabe ukhona ozolukhumbula?

Qala ngokuqonda ukuthi izithameli zakho zingobani: iqembu elithile labantu ibhizinisi lakho lokudla elibasebenzelayo, kanye nenkinga obaxazululela yona. Umkhiqizo wakho kufanele uxhumane nemikhuba yabo, izinto abazithandayo, abangazithandi, kanye nokuphila kwansuku zonke. Ukusuka lapho, yakha isu lomkhiqizo, uhlelo lwemigomo yakho yesikhathi eside nokuthi ibhizinisi lakho lizoyifinyelela kanjani. Izinto ezimbili eziyinhloko zalo inhloso yomkhiqizo (kungani ibhizinisi lakho likhona, futhi lingenza yiphi inzuzo empilweni yezithameli zakho? Lokhu kuba yisitatimende sakho senhloso) kanye nombono womkhiqizo (ulibona kuphi ibhizinisi lakho esikhathini esizayo? Lokhu kubumba imigomo yakho yesikhathi eside) (Yolisa Motha, 2021).

🎯 Strategy Starter (ibheji)

Nika Igama Lomkhiqizo Wakho Izwi Nokukhanga

"Uma ibhizinisi lakho lingakhuluma, ingabe lizohlekisa, noma ligcine izinto zisemthethweni ngokuphelele?"

Uphawu lwakho lwebhizinisi (brand) nalo ludinga ubuntu, ukuze lube ngokoqobo nokuzwana kalula. Cabanga ngendlela ofuna amakhasimende akubone ngayo: onobuntu noma osemthethweni, ojabulisayo noma ozolile, wesimanje noma ogxile emasikweni, odlalayo noma oqinisile, ofinyeleleka kalula noma ophakeme. Kulobo buntu kuvela izwi lohlobo lomkhiqizo wakho, indlela oluphimisa ngayo nomsindo walo, njengokungathi umuntu wangempela ukhuluma nekhasimende. Ekugcineni, umqondo omfushane wohlobo lwakho lomkhiqizo (tagline) ngumugqa omfushane, okhumbulekayo ohlanganisa konke lokhu ngephimbo elilodwa (Yolisa Motha, 2021).

Yakha ukubukeka abantu abakubonayo babone ukuthi nguwe

Ingabe ikhasimende lingayibona yini ipakethe lakho phakathi kwezinye izitendi zokudla eziyishumi, lisuka ngale kwesiteshi samatekisi? Ingabe ikhasimende lingayibona yini ipakethe lakho phakathi kwezinye izitendi zokudla eziyishumi, lisuka ngale kwesiteshi samatekisi?

Ngaphandle kwesu, udinga uphawu lomkhiqizo (brand identity), izici ezibonakalayo ezakha uhlobo lwakho lomkhiqizo. Ilogo yakho iyibuso lebhizinisi lakho, uphawu abantu abaluqaphelayo ngaphambi kokuba bafunde igama lakho; ilogo enhle ilula, iyakhumbuleka, futhi ayibizi kakhulu (ezinye zamalogo amakhulu kakhulu emhlabeni zabiza amarandi ambalwa nje ukuklanywa). Ngisho nemibono ithwele incazelo: izimo eziyindilinga zizwakala zifudumele futhi zimukelekile, izikwele nama-rectangle azwakala eqinile futhi ethembekile, imigqa ephakamile izwakala iqinisekile, imigqa evundlile izwakala izolile. Nombalo wakho nawo ubalulekile, njengoba imibala ivusa imizwa: obomvu udala isifiso sokudla, oluhlaza utshengisa okusha nokunempilo. Qala ngemibala emithathu emikhulu, bese wandisa kusukela lapho. Gcina i-typography yakho, amafonti owasebenzisayo, efana yonke indawo igama lebhizinisi lakho elivela kuyo, kusukela kumenyu wakho kuya kumamediya wakho wezokuxhumana (Yolisa Motha, 2021).
