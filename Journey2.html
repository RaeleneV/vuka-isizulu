Uhambo 02

Gcina Okwengeziwe Kwalokho Okuzuzayo

"Usebenzele wonke amarandi namuhla. Pho, uhhafu wawo uye kuphi ngesikhathi ufika ekhaya?" Lolu hambo lumayelana nokuqonda izinombolo zakho, ukunciphisa izindleko ongazidingi, ukugcina ubufakazi balokho okuzuzayo, nokuthola leyo mali indawo ephephe kunesikhwama sakho.

Yazi Izinombolo Zakho Ezintathu

"Uma othile ebuza ukuthi ibhizinisi lakho lenze malini ngenyanga edlule, ubungaphendula ngenombolo, noma ngokuqagela nje?"

Wonke umthengisi udinga ukwazi izinombolo ezintathu: imali engenayo ephelele (turnover), izindleko nenzuzo. Imali engenayo ephelele yiyo yonke imali engena ekuthengisweni, ngaphambi kokuba kukhishwe izindleko. Izindleko yikho konke okuchithayo: isitoko, ezokuthutha, ukupakisha, izimali zezimvume, idatha ne-airtime. Inzuzo yimali engenayo ephelele ikhishwe izindleko, futhi yiyona yodwa inombolo ekutshela ukuthi wenzeni ngempela.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO: Ekupheleni kwesonto ngalinye, hlanganisa yonke "Imali Engenayo" ne-"Imali Ephumayo," bese ukhipha. Yiyo leyo nzuzo yakho yesonto, ngaphansi kwemizuzu emihlanu.

💰 Ulwazi Lwezinombolo (ibheji)

Ungakuxubi Imali Yebhizinisi Neyakho

"Uma imali yebhizinisi lakho neyokuhamba kwakho ngeteksi ihlala esikhwameni esisodwa, ubungayazi kanjani ukuthi uchitha iyiphi?"

Abathengisi abaningi balahlekelwa yinzuzo ngoba imali yebhizinisi neyomuntu siqu ixutshwe esikhwameni noma epherisini elilodwa. Ukugwema lokhu, gcina izitsha noma ama-akhawunti amabili noma amathathu ahlukene: elilodwa lebhizinisi, elilodwa lokuchitha komuntu siqu noma komndeni, futhi ngokuhle elilodwa lezimo eziphuthumayo. Zama ukuzikhokhela inani elimisiwe, njengomkhokhelo womsebenzi, kunokuthatha imali noma nini lapho uyidinga. Beka eceleni u-10% kuya ku-20% wenzuzo yakho yesonto esitsheni sokonga, ku-stokvel, noma ku-akhawunti futhi, ukuze ube nesivikelo ezinsukwini ezihamba kancane.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO: Phatha "umholo" wakho njengezindleko ezimisiwe zesonto, njengesitoko noma irenti. Vikela inzuzo yakho ekunyamalaleni ngokungabonakali.

Kuhle! Usuhlele izindawo zakho ezintathu zemali.

🐖 Ukuhlukanisa Nokuvikela (ibheji)

Vimbela Ukuvuza

"Ubungenaso isonto elibi lokuthengisa. Pho kungani ugcine unenzuzo encane kunenyanga edlule?"

Amabhizinisi amaningi amancane alahlekelwa imali hhayi ngoba engathengisi ngokwanele, kodwa ngoba izindleko zawo ziphezulu kakhulu noma azilawulwa kahle. Qhathanisa amanani phakathi kwabahlinzeki okungenani ababili ngaphambi kokuzibophezela kumuntu oyedwa njalo. Nciphisa imfucuza, ikakhulukazi ngokudla okusha, ngokuthenga kuphela lokho ongakuthengisa ngempela ngaphambi kokuba kubole. Gwema omashonisa nabanikeli bemali abangabhalisiwe nya: amanani abo enzalo angaphendula isikweletu se-R1,000 sibe ngu-R2,000 noma ngaphezulu okufanele ukukhokhe. Njalo ngenyanga, bheka emuva encwadini yakho yamanothi ubuze ukuthi ingabe kukhona okuthile okushibhile, okuhle ngokulinganayo, ezintweni zakho ezibiza kakhulu.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO: Ukwethembeka kumhlinzeki oyedwa kuhle, kodwa hhayi uma kukubiza imali ngokuthula njalo ngesonto. Kuyafaneleka ukuba nengxoxo enganethezekile yokubuza kwabanye.

✅ INSELELE ESHESHAYO

Vala Ukuvuza

Umsebenzi omuhle. Ukhombile ukuvuza kanye nendlela yokukulungisa.

🔍 Umnciphisi Wezindleko (ibheji)

Gcina Ubufakazi Bawo Wonke Amarandi

"Umuntu onikeza imali ucela ukubona umlando wakho wokuthengisa. Yini ongayikhombisa ngempela njengamanje?"

Kuzwelonke, amabhizinisi amaningi aseNingizimu Afrika angabhalisiwe awagcini amarekhodi ezimali nhlobo. Ngaphandle kwamarekhodi, awukwazi ukufakazela imali oyingenisayo ebhange, awukwazi ukufaka isicelo soxhaso, futhi awukwazi ngempela ukuthi ibhizinisi lakho lenza imali yini. Isixazululo silula: incwadi eyodwa, ehlukaniswe ngokuthi "Imali Engenayo" ne-"Imali Ephumayo." Bhala yonke ithransekshini ngosuku olwenzeka ngalo, ngisho nezincane. Gcina yonke irisidi evela kubahlinzeki noma kumasipala esikhwameni esisodwa sepulasitiki noma efayilini, ihlelwe ngenyanga. Hlanganisa konke njalo ngesonto nangenyanga, lokhu kuba irekhodi lakho lezimali elilula nobufakazi bakho bemali engenayo uma usulungele ukufaka isicelo sosekelo.

📒 Umgcini Wobufakazi (ibheji)

Yibhange, Noma Ungenayo I-CIPC

"Yonke inzuzo yakho isesikhwameni sepulasitiki ngaphansi kombhede wakho. Kwenzekani uma inyamalala kusasa?"

Awudingi ukubhalisa ne-CIPC ukuze uvule i-akhawunti yebhange yebhizinisi njengomnikazi oyedwa. Amabhange amaningana aseNingizimu Afrika anikeza ama-akhawunti akhelwe ngokukhethekile abathengisi abangakabhaliswa. I-Capitec Entrepreneur Account isebenza ngokusekelwe ku-akhawunti yakho ekhona ye-Capitec GlobalOne yomuntu siqu nesazisi sakho saseNingizimu Afrika, ngaphandle kwenani eliphansi lokubeka ukuze uvule. I-TymeBank Everyday Business Account isebenza ngokusekelwe ku-akhawunti yakho ekhona ye-TymeBank "Good Friends" yomuntu siqu, ivulwa mahhala, futhi ihlelwa ku-app ngaphansi kwe-My Profile > My Details > Business Benefits. Ukubeka imali yakho ngokuvamile, ngisho nasesontweni, kwakha umlando wamathransekshini, okuyilokho kanye ozokudinga kamuva ukuze uthole uxhaso noma umshini wamakhadi.
