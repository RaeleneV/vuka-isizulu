Uhambo 03

Yenza Ibhizinisi Lakho Libe Semthethweni

"Cabanga ukuthi i-Pick n Pay ifuna ukuthenga amagwinya akho kusasa. Ulungele?" Iningi labathengi abakhulu, abanikeli bemali, namabhange ngeke basebenze nawe kuze kube ibhizinisi lakho lisemthethweni. Lolu hambo lukuhamba nawe ngqo ukuthi "kusemthethweni" kusho ini, isinyathelo esincane ngasinye. Akukho izinkulumo, nje lokho okufanele ukwenze ngokulandelayo.

Kungani Kufanele Ubhalise?

"Usuthengisa amapuleti angu-100 ngesonto ebaleni lakho. Kungani udinga amaphepha nhlobo?"

Njengamanje, wena nebhizinisi lakho niyinto eyodwa emehlweni omthetho. Uma kuhamba kabi into, kuyinkinga yakho siqu, hhayi nje inkinga "yebhizinisi." Uma usubhalisile, ibhizinisi lakho liba yinto eyodwa yalo, ehlukile kuwe (Companies Act 71 ka-2008). Yilokho okuvula umnyango wama-akhawunti ebhange, izinkontileka ezinkulu, noxhaso.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO Awudingi ukubhalisa konke ngesikhathi esisodwa. Qala ngephepha elilodwa elivula iminyango eminingi kunawo wonke: ukubhaliswa kwakho kwe-CIPC (isifundo esilandelayo).

Ake Silenze Libe Semthethweni

"Othile ubuza: "Ingabe ibhizinisi lakho libhalisiwe?" Wena uthini ngempela?"

I-CIPC (i-Companies and Intellectual Property Commission) yilapho wonke amabhizinisi aseNingizimu Afrika athola khona ubuwena bawo obusemthethweni (Companies Act 71 ka-2008). Ubhalisa ku-inthanethi ku-cipc.co.za. Uma usubhalisiwe, ibhizinisi lakho linegama lalo nobuwena balo obusemthethweni, obuhlukile kuwe siqu.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO Gcina isitifiketi sakho se-CIPC njengesithombe efonini yakho. Uzodinga ukusikhombisa kaningi, ezimvumeni, ebhange, nakubanikeli bemali.

✅ INSELELE ESHESHAYO

Ncwadisa Izimvume Zakho

Hamba ku-cipc.co.za uhlole ukuthi yiziphi izincwadi ozozidinga ngaphambi kokuqala. Zibhale phansi futhi uthole: 📄 ibheji elithi "Kusemthethweni Ngempela".

📄 Kusemthethweni Ngempela (ibheji)

Thola Imvume Yakho Yokudla Ilungiswe

"Ingabe umhloli wezempilo angakuvala kusasa?"

Yebo, uma ungenaso Isitifiketi Sokwamukeleka (esibizwa ngokuthi i-CoA), uthengisa ngokungemthetho, ngisho noma ukudla kwakho kumnandi kakhulu. Le yimvume evela kumasipala wakini ekhombisa ukuthi ikhishi lakho liphephile. Kuyisidingo esisemthethweni ngaphansi koMthethonqubo R638 (R638 ka-2018).

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO I-CoA yakho ihlobene NAWE NEndawo yakho ETHILE. Uma ushintsha izitali noma ushintshana ngezandla, udinga entsha ngokuphelele. Ayidluliselwa.

✅ INSELELE ESHESHAYO

Thola Imvume Yakho

Thola ihhovisi lakho eliseduze lomasipala le-Environmental Health bese ugcina inombolo efonini yakho. Uma usukutholile lokhu, gcwalisa indawo nenombolo yefoni yehhovisi ezikhaleni ngezansi. Qedela lokhu uthole: 🍗 ibheji elithi "Ukudla Okuphephile".

Kuhle! Uyazi ukuthi ungaxhumana kanjani nehhovisi lakho lezempilo lasekhaya.

🍗 Ukudla Okuphephile (ibheji)

Ezinye Izimvume Abantu Abazikhohlwayo

"Usuyithole i-CoA yakho. Ingabe usemthethweni ngokuphelele manje?"

Hhayi ngokuphelele. Amabhizinisi amaningi okudla adinga futhi Ilayisense Yebhizinisi ehlukile evela kumasipala, ngaphansi kwe-Businesses Act (Businesses Act 71 ka-1991). Uma upheka ekhaya, ungase udinge nemvume yokuthi impahla yakho isetshenziselwe ibhizinisi (okubizwa ngokuthi i-zoning). Usebenzisa igesi, noma uhlela ukuthengisa utshwala? Engeza Isitifiketi Segesi Nelayisense Yotshwala ohlwini lwakho nazo.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO Cela umasipala wakho uhlu oluphelele lwezimvume ekuvakasheni kwakho kokuqala. Abantu abaningi bathola ngemvume engekho eyodwa ngesikhathi, ngendlela enzima.

🧾 Uchwepheshe Wamaphepha (ibheji)

Ukulungisa Intela Yakho

"Uma ibhizinisi lakho lenze u-R50,000 ngenyanga edlule, ingabe kukhona ozikweletayo ku-SARS?"

Kuya ngokuthi ibhizinisi lakho lizuza malini ngonyaka. Kusukela ngo-Ephreli 2026, kufanele ubhalise i-VAT kuphela uma uzuza ngaphezu kwe-R2.3 million ngonyaka (SARS, Budget 2026 FAQs). Uma uzuza kancane, uhlelo olulula olubizwa ngokuthi i-Turnover Tax lungase lube ngcono kuwe, njengoba lithatha indawo yezintela eziningana ngokubala okulula okukodwa.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO Ungaqageli inombolo yakho. Hlola i-sars.gov.za noma uvakashele igatsha le-SARS ukuze ubone ukuthi ibhizinisi lakho lihlala kuphi ngempela, njengoba lezi zilinganiso zibuyekezwa ngokuhamba kwesikhathi.

💵 Ohlakaniphile Ngentela (ibheji)

Kusuka Emsebenzini Wokwengeza Kuya Ebhizinisini Elisemthethweni

"Uthengisa esitalini, ebaleni, noma etafuleni lasekhishini lakho njengamanje. Uba kanjani semthethweni ngokuphelele ngempela?"

Akudingeki wenze konke ngesonto elilodwa. Izidingo ziyahluka ngokuya ngokuthi uthengisa kuphi, uthengisani, nokuthi ibhizinisi lakho lihlelwe kanjani, ngakho akukho ukulandelana okulodwa okumisiwe okusebenzela wonke umuntu. Ngokujwayelekile, iningi labantu liqala ngokuhlola ukuthi indawo yabo ihlelwe noma ivunyelwe kahle, bese libhalisa ne-CIPC, bese lifaka izicelo zanoma yiziphi izimvume noma amalayisense adingwa ukuthengisa kwabo (abahloli bavame ukuhlola eziningana ngesikhathi esisodwa), bese belungisa intela uma sebethengisa ngokugcwele. Selokhu ngo-2024, ngisho namashabhu e-spaza kufanele abhalise, ngakho uhulumeni ubhekisisa lolu hlangothi kakhulu kunangaphambili.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO Khetha ISINYATHELO SEKUNYE ukuze usiqedele kuleli sonto. Ungazami ukwenza konke ngesikhathi esisodwa.
