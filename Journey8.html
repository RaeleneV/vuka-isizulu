Uhambo 08

Sebenza Ngobuhlakani

"Usuvele usebenza kanzima. Kodwa ingabe usuku lwakho lukusebenzela ngempela, noma uvele usabele kulokho okwenzeka ngokulandelayo? Lolu hambo luhlanganisa imikhuba yansuku zonke, ukuxhumana, isikhathi, ukusebenzisana, ubungcweti, inethiwekhi yakho, kanye nendlela okhokhelwa ngayo, izinto ezihlukanisa ibhizinisi elisebenza kahle nelibushelelezi kwelinye elivele liqhubeke nje."

Khuluma Namakhasimende Nabahlinzeki Ngendlela Efaneley

"Ikhasimende lithi ukudla kwakusuku olwedlule bekubanda. Uyangena uzokuchaza, noma uvala uze baphothule kuqala?

Indlela okhuluma ngayo kahle ihlobene ngqo nokusebenza kahle kwebhizinisi lakho. Ukuxhumana okuhle kwakha ukwethenjwa, futhi ukwethenjwa yilokho okubuyisela ikhasimende. Bamukele bonke amakhasimende, nje ngokuthi "Sawubona," "Dumela," noma "Good morning" ngaphambi kokukhuluma ngentengo noma ngomkhiqizo kusetha umoya wokuhlonipha. Lalela ngaphambi kokukhuluma: uma ikhasimende likhononda, vumela liphelezele ngaphambi kokuphendula, lokhu kubizwa ngokuthi ukulalela ngokugcwele, futhi kuyikhono elibaluleke kakhulu umnikazi webhizinisi elincane okufanele abe nalo. Caca ngentengo nomkhiqizo njalo, ungalindi ikhasimende liphinde libuze kabili. Futhi gcina indlela efanayo enobungane necacile nabahlinzeki: hlala uqinisekisa ukuthi uyayala ini, kubiza malini, nokuthi uzokhokha noma uyolanda nini.

Isihloko sesibili: Hlela Usuku Lwakho Ukuze Lungakuphathi

"Ekuqaleni kwansuku yakho kwaphi ngempela? Ungakwazi ukuveza amahora wonke alo?"

Akusikho ukuntuleka kolwazi ngomkhiqizo okubambelele abathengisi abaningi baseNingizimu Afrika, kodwa ukuphathwa kwesikhathi. Ngokwesilinganiso, abanikazi bamabhizinisi amancane balahlekelwa cishe imizuzu engu-96 ngosuku ngenxa yesikhathi esingahlelekile noma esilahlakele. Isakhiwo esilula nsuku zonke siyasiza: ngaphambi kokuphuma ekhaya, lungisa isitoko, bala imali, bese uhlole imishini yakho. Fika ngesikhathi esanele ukuze usethe phambi kwehora elimatasa kakhulu. Ngesikhathi sokuhweba, gxila kuphela ekuthengiseni nasekusebenzeleni, ungazenzi izisebenzi zokuphatha phakathi nesikhathi esimatasa. Sebenzisa izikhathi ezithule ukuhambisa isitoko, ukuhlanza, noma ukubuyekeza incwadi yakho yokubhalela. Ekupheleni kosuku, bala imali, bhala phansi okuthengisile nokuchithile, bese uhlela isitoko sosuku olulandelayo.

⏰ Time Tamer (ibheji)

Isihloko sesithathu: Yakha Ithimba, Ngisho Nelincane

"Uma ugula kusasa, umuntu okukusizayo uyokwazi ngempela ukuthi kufanele enzeni?"

Ngisho nendawo yokudayisa enabantu oyedwa noma ababili isizuzisa uma kunezigaba ezicacile zomsebenzi. Abanikazi abanomsizi othembekile, ngisho nosizo olungeyona eyezomthetho oluvela emndenini, bangachitha isikhathi esiningi ezintweni eziyikhulisa ibhizinisi ngempela, njengokuthola isitoko esingcono noma ukuphenya izindawo zokudayisa ezintsha. Uma usebenza nomunye umuntu oyedwa kuphela, vumelana ngokusobala, ungacabangi nje, ukuthi ubani opheka, ubani okhonza, futhi ubani ophatha imali. Yiba nomuntu oyedwa ocacile "wemali" ngaso sonke isikhathi, ukuvimbela ukudideka nokungathembani. Bonga futhi ubonise ukuhlonipha umsebenzi omuhle, akubizi lutho futhi kugcina abasizi bejabulile. Uma kwenzeka ukhula unaphezu komuntu oyedwa noma ababili abasizi, vumelana ngemithetho elula, enobulungiswa kusengaphambili: isikhathi sokuqala nesokuphela, ukuthi umholo noma inzuzo ihlukaniswa kanjani, nokuthi kwenzekeni uma umuntu engaveli.

Ziphathe Njengochwepheshe Osevele Uyibo

"Ingabe usomabhizinisi kamasipala, umphakeli, nomthengi wokuqala bazobheka ibhizinisi lakho ngokungathi ngeliqondile namuhla?"

Cishe ama-70% amabhizinisi amancane eNingizimu Afrika ayawa phakathi nonyaka wawo wokuqala, ngokuvamile ngenxa yokuntuleka kokuvumelana kanye nokusebenza kobungcweti, hhayi imikhiqizo emibi. Ubungcweti yikona okwenza abantu bakuthathe ngokungathi sína ngisho noma ungenasitolo esiphambili. Thengisa endaweni efanayo, ngezikhathi ezifanayo, ngangokunokwenzeka, amakhasimende abuyela kulokho angathembela kukho. Gcina izandla zakho, izitsha, kanye nesitolo kuhlanzekile, okuyimfuneko yokuhlanzeka okusemthethweni. Gcina amanani akho elungile futhi ehambisanayo kusukela kumakhasimende kuya kumakhasimende. Uma kukhona okungahambi kahle, xolisa futhi unikeze ikhambi elifanele ngokuthula, kunokuba uphikisane esidlangalaleni. Futhi gcina izwi lakho: uma utshela umhlinzeki noma ikhasimende isikhathi, intengo, noma inani, lihloniphe. Ukuthembeka yidumela lakho.

Yakha Inethiwekhi Ekusekelayo

"Ubani ongakuxwayisa uma kukhona umhloli oza kuwe ntambama namuhla?"

Abahwebi abanamanethiwekhi abanzi, abanye abahwebi, abaphakeli, oxhumana nabo bomphakathi, bathola ukufinyelela okungcono kolwazi, uxhaso, kanye nokusekelwa ngezikhathi ezinzima kunalabo abasebenza bodwa. Lokhu kuyiqiniso ikakhulukazi emarenki amatekisi, lapho abathengisi abaningi behwebelana khona. Yazi abahwebi abakuzungezile: abagcini nje ngokuba abancintisani, bangabelana ngoxhumana nabo abaphakeli, bakuxwayise ngokuhlolwa, babheke isitolo sakho, noma bathumele amakhasimende amaningi ngendlela yakho. Joyina noma wakhe iqembu labahwebi, amarenki amaningi amatekisi kanye namadolobhana asevele enezinhlangano ezingakahleleki; ihhovisi lezokuthuthukiswa komnotho likamasipala wakho livame ukukutshela ukuthi likhona yini eduze. Gcina oxhumana nabo abaphakeli belondolozwe ocingweni lwakho ngamanothi ngamanani kanye nokuthembeka, futhi ube khona emicimbini yendawo yamahhala eqhutshwa yi-SEDA noma umasipala wakho uma ukwazi. Amaqembu e-WhatsApp ayindlela yamahhala, engabizi yokuhlala exhumene nawo wonke umuntu ngesikhathi esisodwa.

🌐 Exhumene Kahle (ibheji)

Yamukela Izinkokhelo Zekhadi ne-Yoco

"Ikhasimende lifuna ukuthepha ikhadi lalo, kodwa uthatha imali kuphela. Ingabe liyathenga noma kunjalo, noma liyahamba liye esitebeleni esilandelayo?"

Cishe amabhizinisi amancane aseNingizimu Afrika ayizigidi eziyisithupha asamukela imali kuphela, kodwa amakhasimende amaningi manje aphatha imali encane, ekhathazekile ngokweba. Ukwamukela imali kanye nekhadi kukunikeza okuhle kakhulu, futhi awudingi ukubhaliswa yi-CIPC ukuze ubhalise ne-Yoco. Iya ku-yoco.com noma ulande uhlelo lokusebenza lwe-Yoco, khetha "Qala," bese ukhetha i-Sole Proprietor. Faka imininingwane yakho yomuntu siqu bese uqinisekisa ubuwena bakho ngesithombe se-ID yakho yaseNingizimu Afrika. Faka ikheli lakho lokuhweba (ikheli lakho lasekhaya lilungile uma uhweba kusukela lapho) kanye ne-akhawunti yasebhange yaseNingizimu Afrika egameni lakho. Khetha umshini wakho wekhadi, i-Yoco Go esezingeni lokuqala ibiza cishe u-R800 kusengaphambili ngemali encane yansuku zonke kuphela ezinsukwini oyisebenzisayo, futhi ayikho imali yanyanga zonke. Uma isifikile, isivele ixhunywe kuphrofayela yakho.

💡 ICEBISO ELISHESHAYO: Izinkokhelo zamakhadi zifika ku-akhawunti yakho ngosuku olulandelayo lwebhizinisi, hhayi ngokushesha, ngakho-ke hlela ukuhamba kwemali yakho ngaleyo ndlela uma udinga imali ekhona ukuze uthenge isitoko.
