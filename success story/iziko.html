Kusuka Ekusindeni Kuya Empumelelweni: Indaba kaBuyiswa Tembani ne-Iziko African Cuizine

Iziko African Cuizine • Entrepreneurship Story • Eastern Cape

Lokho okwaqala njengendlela yokusinda ngemuva kokulahlekelwa umsebenzi ngo-2009 sekukhule kwaba ibhizinisi eliphumelelayo lokudla nokuphatha imicimbi.

KuBuyiswa, umsunguli we-Iziko African Cuizine, ubusomabhizinisi abuzange bube yingxenye yomsebenzi ohlelwe ngokucophelela. Kunalokho, kwazalwa isidingo, ukuzimisela nokulungela ukubona amathuba uma evela.

Namuhla, i-Iziko African Cuizine isebenza e-Uitenhage, e-Despatch, eGqeberha (ePort Elizabeth), eKirkwood, e-Addo nase-Jeffreys Bay, inezinhloso zokwandela ezindaweni ezizungezile ezengeziwe. Kodwa uhambo lokufika lapho lwalungelula neze.

Kusuka Ekulahlekelweni Umsebenzi Kuya Ekutholeni Ithuba

UBuyiswa wakhulela e-Uitenhage eMpumalanga Kapa emndenini ongenamnotho, kodwa lapho abazali bakhe baqinisekisa ukuthi izingane zabo zinakho okudingekayo empilweni. Imfundo nesonto kwakubekwa phambili emzini wakhe, yizindinganiso akholelwa ukuthi zibe nendima ebalulekile ekwakheni umuntu aseyiyena namuhla.

Esikoleni, uBuyiswa ubevame ukuba ngomunye wabaphumelela kakhulu ebangeni lakhe. Kodwa-ke, ngaphandle komeluleki wokumhlahla, wazithola eseyeka ukugxila ngesikhathi ebangeni le-11 nele-12. Naphezu kwalokho, akazange ahluleke unyaka.

Ngemva kwesikole, wabhalisela enyuvesi ukuze afunde i-BCom Computer Science. Kodwa-ke, izinselele zezimali nengcindezi yekhosi ekugcineni kwamenza wayishiya inyuvesi. Waphinde wahlangabezana nenselele yokungathola usizo lwezimali oluvela ku-Tertiary Education Fund of South Africa (TEFSA), okwaba nomthelela esinqumweni sakhe sokuyeka izifundo zakhe.

Eminyakeni eyalandela, ngo-2009, uBuyiswa walahlekelwa umsebenzi. Ngaleso sikhathi, wayenosana namadodakazi amabili ayesafunda esikoleni. Njengoba kwakunzima ukuthola umsebenzi emakethe, wadinga ukuthola indlela yokondla umndeni wakhe.

"Ngacabanga ngesixazululo esisheshayo ukuze sikwazi ukudla futhi ngikwazi ukuqhubeka nokukhokhela izikole zabo," kuchaza yena.

Ngokusebenzisa izinsiza ebezitholakala kuye, uBuyiswa waqala ikhefi le-inthanethi nebhizinisi lokuphrinta. Ngaleso sikhathi, wayengazi ukuthi lesi sixazululo sesikhashana ekugcineni sasizoba yisisekelo sohambo lwakhe lobusomabhizinisi.

Ukuthola Isidingo Sokudla

Yize ibhizinisi lakhe lokuqala lalingelona ibhizinisi lokudla, uBuyiswa kungekudala wabona ithuba.

Kwakukhula isidingo sokudla emakhasimendeni, futhi waqala ukuzama ukunikeza okuncane. Waqala ngokuthengisa ama-scone nekhofi ngaphambi kokwethula ubhiya wejinja. Njengoba amakhasimende eya ngokuya enentshisekelo ekudleni kwakhe, aqala ukucela ukudla okuphekwe ekhaya.

Lesi sidingo saguqula isiqondiso sebhizinisi lakhe.

Lokho okwaqala njengomsebenzi omncane wokuthengisa ukudla ngamanani athengekayo ekugcineni kwakhula kwaba yi-Iziko African Cuizine. UBuyiswa ukhumbula ukuthi amanani akhe okuqala ayengaba ngu-R20 ngepuleti. Namuhla, isidingo nenani lokudla kwakhe kumvumele ukuthi akhokhise cishe u-R180 ngepuleti kwezinye zezinto anikeza zona.

Ukukhula akuzange kwenzeke ngobusuku obubodwa. Kwaqhutshwa ukulalela amakhasimende, ukubona lokho abantu ababekufuna nokuvumelanisa izinsizakalo zakhe njalo.

Ukukhula Ngokungaguquki

Lapho ebuzwa ukuthi waphumelela kanjani ukukhulisa ibhizinisi kusukela ekuqaleni kwalo okuthobekile, uBuyiswa ukhomba umgomo owodwa ngaphezu kwayo yonke: ukungaguquki.

Ukholelwa ukuthi amakhasimende akathengi nje umkhiqizo. Athenga isipiliyoni sonke namagugu aphelezela ibhizinisi.

"Amakhasimende awathengi umkhiqizo kuphela kodwa iphakheji eza nawo," kusho yena.

Ngisho nangezikhathi ebezizwa ukuthi ibhizinisi lihlangabezana nobunzima, wenqaba ukwehlisa izinga lezinsizakalo zakhe noma izimiso zakhe zebhizinisi. Ikhono lakhe lokuhlala engaguquki ngenkathi evumelanisa nezinguquko emakethe laba enye yezisekelo ze-Iziko African Cuizine.

Namuhla, ibhizinisi selikhule lidlula ekunikezeni nje ukudla. Selibe yinkampani yokudla nokuphatha imicimbi, kanti uBuyiswa uqhubeka efuna izindlela ezintsha zokungena emakethe nokukhulisa ibhizinisi.

Ukubhalisa Ibhizinisi Ngokusemthethweni

UBuyiswa ekuqaleni wasebenza ngokungakabhaliswa ngemva kokuqala ibhizinisi ngo-2009. Eminyakeni emithathu kamuva, ngoJulayi 2012, walibhalisa ngokusemthethweni ibhizinisi.

Isinqumo sokubhalisa i-Iziko African Cuizine ngokusemthethweni sasithonywe kakhulu ukukhula kwebhizinisi nokwanda kwesidingo sezinsizakalo zakhe.

Njengoba amakhasimende eqala ukufaka ama-oda amakhulu futhi kuvela amathuba okungena ezinkontilekeni, ukusebenza ngokungakabhaliswa kungabe kwakhawulela ukukhula kwenkampani.

"Ngangingakwazi ukunikeza noma ukungena ezinkontilekeni ngaphandle kwebhizinisi elibhalisiwe, futhi lokho bekuzovimbela ukukhula kwami," kuchaza yena.

Ukubhalisa ibhizinisi ngokusemthethweni kwaphinde kwavumela uBuyiswa ukuthi athole amathuba okuqeqeshwa anikezwa ngezinhlelo zikahulumeni nezomasipala.

Yize echaza inqubo yokubhalisa uqobo lwayo njengeyelula ngokuqhathaniswa, ukhumbula ukuthi inqubo yathatha isikhathi eside ngoba wasebenzisa izinsizakalo zokwesekwa nguhulumeni ngokusebenzisa i-Small Enterprise Development Agency (SEDA).

KuBuyiswa, ukubekezela kwakubalulekile.

"Into eyodwa eyayikhathaza kwakuwukudingeka ngilandelele ku-SEDA," kusho yena.

Naphezu kwezinkinga zokubambezeleka, akazange acabange ngokuyeka.

"Angiyena umuntu oqala izinto angazifezi."

Ukufunda Ngenkathi Eqhubeka

Enye yezinto ezithakazelisa kakhulu ohambweni lukaBuyiswa ukuthi akaqalanga nolwazi olubanzi lwebhizinisi noma umeluleki omhlahla esinyathelweni ngasinye.

Kunalokho, wafunda ngokuhlangenwe nakho.

Wasebenzisa i-inthanethi, wabamba iqhaza kuma-workshop amabhizinisi amancane futhi wasebenzisa izinsizakalo ze-SEDA lapho ebhalisa ibhizinisi lakhe. Waphinde wabuka amabhizinisi asebenza ngezinga elikhulu futhi wafunda kulokho ayekwenza.

Kunokubona ukungabi nolwazi kwakhe njengesithiyo, uBuyiswa wamukela indlela yokucabanga yokufunda njalo.

Selokhu abambe iqhaza ezinhlelweni eziningana zokuthuthukisa nezokukhulisa amabhizinisi (incubation), okubandakanya uhlelo lwezinyanga eziyi-18 nge-The Hope Factory, isikhungo sebhizinisi esinikezwa yi-UDDI, futhi, ngo-2026, uhlelo lokukhulisa amabhizinisi olunikezwa yi-Business Hub Africa eNyuvesi yaseNelson Mandela.

Isipiliyoni sakhe simfundisile ukuthi akukho sikhathi lapho usomabhizinisi angathi uyakwazi konke ngebhizinisi.

"Awukwazi neze ukuthi uyayazi imakethe," kuchaza yena. "Iyashintsha futhi kunjalo nangezici zamakhasimende nesidingo sezinye izinto."

Ukuqonda Imithetho Yemboni Yokudla

Nakuba uBuyiswa etholile inqubo evamile yokubhalisa ibhizinisi lakhe ilula ngokuqhathaniswa, uyavuma ukuthi bekukhona izinto eziningi ayengazazi ekuqaleni ngokusebenza ngaphakathi embonini yokudla.

Phakathi kwezidingo ahlangana nazo kwakukhona izitifiketi zempilo, amalayisense ebhizinisi, Izitifiketi Zokwamukeleka (Certificates of Acceptability) nezimvume ezidingekayo ukuze kusetshenzwe emicimbini ethile.

Lezi zidingo zikhombise ukubaluleka kokuqonda imithethonqubo esebenza embonini ethile ngaphambi kokungena kuyo.

Kosomabhizinisi bokudla abafisa ukuqala, uBuyiswa ugcizelela kakhulu ukubaluleka kokuphepha kokudla.

"Ungaphakeli ukudla okudala noma usebenzise imikhiqizo esiphelelwe yisikhathi," kuxwayisa yena.

Kuye, ukugcina izindinganiso eziphakeme akukhona nje ukuthobela imithethonqubo. Kuyingxenye yokuvikela amakhasimende nokwakha ibhrandi elethenjwayo.

Ukuba Yibhrandi

Naphezu kwezinselele ahlangabezane nazo, uBuyiswa uthi akazisoli ngendlela uhambo lwakhe olwenzeke ngayo.

Ukholelwa ukuthi ikhono lakhe lokuqhubeka nokusebenza nokukhulisa ibhizinisi esimweni sezomnotho esinzima eNingizimu Afrika kungumphumela womusa kaNkulunkulu, ukuphikelela kanye nokuzimisela kwakhe ukuqhubeka efunda.

Ukholelwa nokuthi osomabhizinisi kudingeka bazihlukanise nabanye.

"Wonke umuntu uthengisa ukudla, hluka futhi ube ngowodwa," kweluleka yena. "Yiba nalokho okukodwa abantu abazofika ngakho njalo."

KuBuyiswa, ukwakha ibhrandi kudlula ukuba nelogo noma igama lebhizinisi. Kuwukuhlotshaniswa eduze nebhizinisi nesipiliyoni amakhasimende asitholayo.

"Zakhele ibhrandi. Yiba yibhizinisi lakho."

Iseluleko Kosomabhizinisi Bokudla Abafisa Ukuqala

Kubantu abasebenzisa izitolo zokudla, amabhizinisi asekhaya noma amabhizinisi amancane okudla, iseluleko sikaBuyiswa silula kodwa sinamandla: qala ngalokho onakho.

"Kufanele uthande lokho okwenzayo. Ungazitholi usukhululekile, funda, funda futhi funda."

Ukhuthaza osomabhizinisi abafisa ukuqala ukuthi baqonde ukuthi amakhasimende abo afunani bese bethola izindlela zokunikeza leyo nsizakalo ngokwehlukile kubancintisi babo.

Ukholelwa nokuthi osomabhizinisi akufanele bavumele izikhubekiso zibenze bacabange ukuthi bahlulekile.

"Qala ngalokho onakho. Into ebalulekile ukuthi uqale."

Uhambo lwakhe uqobo luwubufakazi balo mqondo. Lokho okwaqala njengomzamo wokunikeza ukudla nokugcina izingane zakhe zisesikoleni ekugcineni kwaba ibhizinisi elibhalisiwe elinezinsizakalo eziningi nesizinda samakhasimende esandayo.

Ukubheka Phambili

Uhambo lukaBuyiswa lobusomabhizinisi luseqhubeka nokuthuthuka. Yize i-Iziko African Cuizine isikhule kakhulu kusukela ekuqaleni kwayo, ukholelwa ukuthi kusekhona indawo yokwandiswa.

Ibhizinisi lakhe okwamanje lisebenzela amakhasimende e-Uitenhage, e-Despatch, eGqeberha, eKirkwood, e-Addo nase-Jeffreys Bay, futhi unethemba lokwandisa ukufinyelela kwakhe ezindaweni eziseduze ezengeziwe.

Uma ebheka emuva, uhambo lwakhe alubheki njengochungechunge lwamaphutha okwakufanele agwenywe, kodwa njengeqoqo lezipiliyoni ezasiza ukwakha ibhizinisi analo namuhla.

Akaqalanga ngohlelo lwebhizinisi oluningiliziwe, uxhaso lwezimali olubanzi noma umeluleki. Waqala ngesidingo, izinsiza ayenazo nokulungela ukusebenza.

Indaba yakhe ikhombisa ukuthi ubusomabhizinisi abuqali njalo ngomcabango ophelele. Kwesinye isikhathi, buqala ngesidingo. Usomabhizinisi angaqala ngensizakalo eyodwa, athole elinye ithuba futhi kancane kancane akhe okuthile okukhulu kakhulu kunalokho ayekucabanga ekuqaleni.

KuBuyiswa, isihluthulelo sibe ukwenqaba ukuma endaweni eyodwa.

"Ungalokothi uyeke uma iminyango ingavuleki. Hlala uwuqiniso futhi khanya ekhoneni lakho. Ekugcineni leyo minyango izovuleka."

Kusukela ekhefini le-inthanethi nensizakalo yokuphrinta eyavela ngenxa yesidingo kuya ku-Iziko African Cuizine, inkampani ekhulayo yokudla nokuphatha imicimbi, indaba kaBuyiswa ekugcineni ikhuluma ngokuqina, ukuzivumelanisa nokufunda njalo.

Futhi mhlawumbe okubaluleke kakhulu, uhambo lwakhe lukhombisa ukuthi kwesinye isikhathi ibhizinisi olaqalayo alisona ngempela elo ozogcina ulakhile.
