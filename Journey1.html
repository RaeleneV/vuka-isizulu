Uhambo 01

Ingabe Ibhizinisi Lakho Selilungele Ukukhula?

"Uyacabanga ngesitali sesibili, isitoko esengeziwe, noma umsizi wakho wokuqala. Kodwa ukwazi kanjani ngempela ukuthi ulungele, noma uthemba nje?" Ukukhula ngaphambi kwesikhathi, ngaphambi kokuba isisekelo sakho siqine, kungangenisa umthengisi ezikweletini noma kumcindezele. Lolu hambo lukusiza uhlole izimpawu zakho ngaphambi kokuba uchithe noma iyiphi irandi eyengeziwe.

Izimpawu Ezinhlanu Zokuthi Ulungele Ngempela

"Wonke umthengisi uphupha ngokwanda. Kodwa bangaki empeleni abahlola kuqala ukuthi isikhathi sifanele?"

Abeluleki bebhizinisi babonisa isethi ehambisanayo yezimpawu ezikhombisa ukuthi ibhizinisi linesisekelo esiqinile ngokwanele sokukhula ngokuphephile. Imali oyingenisayo iyazinza noma iyakhula phakathi nezinyanga eziningana, hhayi nje ngesonto elihle elilodwa nelilodwa. Inani eliphezulu lamakhasimende akho liyabuya njalo, okukhombisa ukwethenjwa kwangempela. Uvamise ukuthengisa konke noma udinga ukwenqaba amakhasimende, okusho ukuthi kunesidingo esikhulu kunalokho okwamanje ongasanelisa. Amarekhodi akho encwadi yamanothi ahleliwe, ngakho ungabona iphethini yenzuzo yakho ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. Futhi unokonga noma imali yesivikelo, ngakho awuncikile kunoma iyiphi irandi yenzuzo yanamuhla ukuze ubuyisele isitoko kusasa.

Ukuthi "Ukukhula" Kubukeka Kanjani Ngempela

"Ukukhula akusho njalo isitolo esisha sonke. Iyiphi isinyathelo esincane esilandelayo esingakuhambisa phambili ngempela?"

Ukukhula akudingeki kusho isitali sesibili ngokushesha. Kumthengisi wokudla, kungabukeka njengokwengeza umkhiqizo omusha, ukwandisa amahora akho okuthengisa ukuze ube nokuguquguquka, ukuqasha umsizi, noma, ekugcineni, ukuvula indawo yesibili. Lapho isinyathelo sincane, kuncane nobungozi uma kungahambi njengoba kuhlelwe ngqo.

💡 ITHIPHU ESHESHAYO Fanisa isinyathelo sakho sokukhula nesimpawu sakho esiqine kakhulu. Uma uqhubeka uthengisa konke, ukwandisa amahora akho noma ukwengeza isitoko kuwukuhamba kokuqala okuphephe kakhulu kunokuqasha isitali sesibili.
